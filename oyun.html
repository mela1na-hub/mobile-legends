About the Game

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) is a 5v5 MOBA game where two teams battle to destroy the enemy base while defending their own. Teamwork, strategy, and skill are the keys to victory!

  • DeveloperMoonton
  • Release DateJuly 14, 2016
  • Game Mode5v5
  • PlatformAndroid, iOS

5 steps to victory

  1. 1

  2. 2

Game Overview

Land of Dawn map

Three lanes, jungle, Turtle, Lord — know the battlefield.

  1. 1
    Base (Spawn)

    Revives heroes.

  2. 2
    Top Lane (EXP Lane)

    Usually for Fighters/Tanks.

  3. 3
    Mid Lane

    Short lane, faster EXP.

  4. 4
    Bottom Lane (Gold Lane)

    For Marksmen.

  5. 5
    Jungle

    Monsters give buffs & gold.

  6. 6
    Turtle

    Provides team gold & EXP.

  7. 7
    Lord

    Strong ally that pushes lanes.

  8. 8
    Turrets

    Defend your lanes.

  9. 9
    Inhibitor Turret

    Destroy to open the base.

  10. 10
    Base (Enemy)

    Destroy to win!
